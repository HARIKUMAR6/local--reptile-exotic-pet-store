Our Approach to Care

The animal's needs come first.

Research species-specific requirements
Good care starts with understanding the particular species — its natural environment, diet, behaviour and lifespan.
Treat welfare as the priority
Decisions about housing, equipment and handling should be judged by what is best for the animal, not by convenience or appearance.
Source responsibly and know the law
We encourage people to ask where animals come from and to check the regulations that apply where they live, as these differ between places.
Respect that every species is different
A setup that suits one animal may be unsuitable for another, even a closely related one.

Habitat & Product Philosophy

The right equipment is whatever suits the animal, its enclosure and the conditions it needs.

The species

Its natural climate, size and behaviour determine what it needs to thrive.

The enclosure

Size, materials and ventilation affect which heating, lighting and substrate will work.

The environment

Room temperature and seasonal changes influence how equipment performs over time.

Our catalogue illustrates the types of equipment we can advise on. Availability varies, and no single product suits every species or setup.

Education & Support

Learn first, then ask.

This website is designed around learning and enquiry. You can read the care guides, browse habitat equipment, and contact us with questions about products and general care resources.

How we can help

  • Introductory care guides by topic
  • Information on habitat equipment types
  • Answers to product and setup enquiries

When to look further

  • Detailed needs of a specific species
  • Health concerns, illness or injury
  • Legal questions about keeping a species

Veterinary advice: our guidance is general and educational. For health questions, consult a qualified veterinarian experienced with exotic animals. For legal questions, check with the relevant local authority.
