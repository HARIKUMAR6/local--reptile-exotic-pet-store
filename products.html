Habitat Essentials

Build a Habitat That Works

Good equipment works as a system. These four elements shape almost every enclosure — the right version of each depends on the species you keep.

01

Temperature & Lighting

Many reptiles rely on an external heat source and a warm-to-cool gradient. Control heaters with a thermostat, check readings where the animal rests, and provide UVB and a steady day–night cycle where the species needs them.

Heating & temperature Lighting
02

Substrate & Environment

Substrate, humidity and ventilation work together. Choose ground cover that suits how the species lives, then monitor humidity and keep air moving so conditions stay fresh rather than stagnant.

Substrate
03

Hides & Enrichment

Secure hides on warmer and cooler sides, sturdy climbing routes and natural décor give animals choice — and encourage behaviours like climbing, exploring and resting out of sight.

Hides & enrichment
04

Water & Maintenance

Provide water in the form the species uses — a stable dish for drinking or soaking, or droplets for animals that drink from leaves. Clean dishes regularly, spot-clean waste and keep simple tools for routine care.

Responsible Product Selection

Start with the animal, not the equipment.

Find a species care guide

  • Match the species

    Temperature, humidity and lighting requirements differ widely. Confirm your animal's needs before choosing equipment.

  • Fit the enclosure

    Heat sources, lighting and substrate depth should suit the size and layout of the enclosure they are going into.

  • Measure and adjust

    Monitor conditions with reliable gauges and adjust over time, especially as seasons and room temperatures change.
