Build a Better Habitat

Six elements every enclosure is built from.

The right combination depends on the species — its adult size, natural climate and behaviour. Use these as a checklist, then confirm the specifics in a species care guide.

01 Suitable enclosure
Sized for the adult animal and oriented to how it lives — tall for climbers, long for ground-dwellers — with secure ventilation.
02 Temperature
A warmer zone and a cooler retreat so the animal can regulate its own temperature, with heat sources controlled and monitored.
03 Lighting
A consistent day–night cycle, plus UVB lighting for species that require it.
04 Substrate
Chosen to suit the species' humidity needs and digging behaviour, and practical to keep clean.
05 Hides
Secure retreats where the animal can rest out of view — ideally on both the warmer and cooler sides.
06 Climbing & enrichment
Branches, ledges, plants and varied textures that encourage natural movement and exploration.

Responsible Ownership

Research first. The right animal is one you can care for well.

Exotic animals differ enormously in what they need. Before acquiring any animal, take time to understand the commitment involved.

Start with our care guides
  1. 01

    Species-specific needs

    Diet, temperature, humidity, space and social needs vary between species — sometimes even between closely related ones.

  2. 02

    Local regulations

    Some species are restricted or prohibited in certain countries, states or municipalities. Check what applies where you live, including any permits.

  3. 03

    Responsible sourcing

    Ask where an animal comes from. Captive-bred animals from reputable, documented sources are generally preferable to wild-caught ones.

  4. 04

    Long-term commitment

    Many exotic species live for a decade or more. Plan for ongoing costs, specialist veterinary care and cover when you are away.
