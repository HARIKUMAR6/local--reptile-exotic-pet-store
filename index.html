Build the Right Habitat

Every species deserves a habitat suited to it.

A successful enclosure is never one-size-fits-all. The right setup depends on the species you're caring for — and getting each element right matters.

  • Enclosure size & shape — matched to adult size, activity level and climbing or burrowing needs.
  • Temperature & heating — an accurate gradient with a warm basking zone and a cooler retreat.
  • Lighting — appropriate UVB and photoperiod for the species' natural rhythm.
  • Humidity — monitored and maintained within the range each species requires.
  • Substrate & enrichment — materials and features that support natural behavior.
